# Safari Shule privacy policy draft

**Effective date:** [DATE]

**Publisher:** [LEGAL PUBLISHER NAME]

Safari Shule is an educational app for young children. The learning experience works without an account. Progress, stars, language preference, and settings are stored on the device.

## Information used by the app

Safari Shule does not ask a child for a name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera access, or microphone access. The app does not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics.

If an adult chooses to make or restore an in-app purchase, Apple App Store or Google Play processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Safari Shule sends the store purchase record and platform name to the publisher's secure verification service to confirm the entitlement. The service must retain only the information needed for fraud prevention, entitlement support, accounting, and legal obligations. [UPDATE THIS PARAGRAPH TO MATCH THE FINAL SERVER IMPLEMENTATION.]

## Device storage

Learning progress and preferences remain on the device. An adult can delete that progress from the Parents area. Removing the app also removes locally stored app data, subject to device backup behavior controlled by Apple or Google.

## Sharing

The publisher does not sell children's personal information. Service providers may process limited purchase-verification data only to operate the optional adult purchase and must be contractually restricted from using it for advertising or unrelated purposes. Safari Shule is designed to minimize data collection. Adult-only settings, external links, and purchases are protected by a parental gate. A parent or guardian may contact the publisher to ask a privacy question or exercise rights available under applicable law.
