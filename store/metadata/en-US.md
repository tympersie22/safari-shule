## Full description

Safari Shule turns early learning into an illustrated adventure inspired by East African places and everyday life.

Children can explore five learning worlds, count objects at the market, practise shapes and colours, complete a three-part Star Test, match memory cards, sort by changing rules, build a village, and play a Stop & Go reaction game.

Choose English or Swahili for a consistent experience throughout the app. Correct answers celebrate and move to the next question automatically, while gentle retry feedback keeps children learning at their own pace.

Designed for families:

- No child account required
- Learning progress is stored on the device
- No advertising or social features
- Adult settings and optional purchases use a parental gate
- Core learning activities work offline

Safari Shule is an educational play experience. Curriculum and language claims in the final listing must match the completed educator and native-speaker review.
